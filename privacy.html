A NOTE ON YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy Notice.

WEBSITE CONTACT INFORMATION / NOVEMBER 2026

KV AGENCY is an independent digital studio. This notice describes how personal information submitted through this website's project inquiry form is handled. The project inquiry form is handled through Formspree.

Information we request

When you choose to submit an inquiry, the form requests your name, email address, project type, estimated budget, and project description. You may also provide your company name. Please do not send passwords, payment card numbers, or sensitive personal information through this form.

How the information is used

The information is used to review your inquiry, respond to your message, discuss potential services, and maintain relevant business correspondence. It is not collected to sell lists of contacts.

Form delivery and service providers

KV AGENCY uses Formspree to process and deliver website inquiries. Formspree may process submitted data under its own privacy terms.

Hosting and technical information

The website hosting provider may process technical request information, such as IP addresses and request logs, according to its own privacy terms. This site does not include advertising pixels, third-party analytics, or marketing cookies by default.

Access, correction, or deletion

If you have contacted us and want to ask about your information, please email hello.kvstudio@gmail.com. Requests will be assessed according to applicable law and relevant business or legal obligations.

Changes to this notice

We may revise this notice if our services, hosting, or data handling change. Any material changes should be reflected here before they take effect.
