The process

A clear path to launch.

See what we’ll need from you and what you’ll get to review at each stage.

01Define the scope

We learn about your business, audience, and goals, then put the deliverables, price, and timeline in writing.

02Review the design

You review the page structure, visual direction, and key calls to action before we build the full site.

03Test the preview

Try the site on your own devices. We check navigation, forms, mobile layouts, and basic accessibility with you before launch.

04Launch and hand over

After your approval, we help connect your domain, check the site again, and hand over the access and notes agreed in the proposal.
